# FAQ

## Does this unlock Spotify Premium?

No.

## Do I need Spotify Premium to use it?

No.

## Does it work with YouTube Music or other music apps?

No.

## How do I install Spicy EX?

Download an APK from [Releases](https://github.com/amarinne/spicy-ex/releases). For rooted phones, enable it in LSPosed, scope Spotify, and restart Spotify. For non-rooted phones, use [LSPatch](https://github.com/JingMatrix/LSPatch).

## Which apps need an LSPosed scope?

Spotify is required. Add Android Auto only when using Android Auto lyrics.

## Does Android Auto work without root?

Android Auto requires root. If you find a way to make it work without root, I would love to hear about it.

## Does LSPatch work?

Yes, but Spotify login can require the downgrade → login → upgrade flow.

## Why are lyrics missing or out of sync?

Availability and timing depend on the track and lyric source. Try another source or adjust the sync offset in Spicy EX settings.

## How do I enable SpicyLyrics.org?

Add [Spicy EX from the catalog](https://developers.spicylyrics.org/catalog/spicy-ex), copy its client key into Spicy EX settings, and enable the source.

## Can I use it offline?

Yes, for lyrics already saved on your device.

## Can I import or correct lyrics myself?

No.

## What survives an update, reinstall, or move to another phone?

Spicy EX stores its data inside Spotify.

- **Updating Spotify or Spicy EX:** settings, saved lyrics, AI results, and keys stay.
- **Reinstalling Spicy EX:** the data stays while Spotify's app data remains.
- **Uninstalling Spotify or clearing its app data:** the data is removed.
- **Moving to another phone:** settings and saved results depend on a Spotify app-data restore. Enter your API keys again.

## Can I suggest language-specific AI instructions?

Yes. Share your prompt or suggestions in the [Telegram group](https://t.me/spicyex), with the source and target languages and a short example.
For Vietnamese, useful guidance includes consistent pronouns and speaker roles supported by the lyric text.
Suggestions for other languages are welcome. You can try them with the [AI custom instructions](FEATURES_USER.md#ai).
